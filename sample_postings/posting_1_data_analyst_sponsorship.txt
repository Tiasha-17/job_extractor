Data Analyst — Northgate Retail Group
Location: London, UK (Hybrid — 3 days in office)
Employment type: Full-time
Salary: £30,000 - £36,000 depending on experience

About the role:
We're looking for a Junior-to-Mid Data Analyst to join our commercial analytics
team. You'll build dashboards, support pricing decisions, and work closely with
the merchandising team to turn sales data into recommendations.

Requirements (essential):
- 1-2 years' experience in a data or business analyst role
- Strong SQL skills
- Experience building dashboards in Power BI or Tableau
- Comfortable presenting findings to non-technical stakeholders

Nice to have:
- Exposure to Python or R for data cleaning
- Experience with e-commerce or retail data
- Familiarity with A/B testing

We welcome applications from candidates who require visa sponsorship — Northgate
is a licensed UK Skilled Worker visa sponsor and has sponsored several analyst
hires in the past year.

Benefits: 25 days holiday, pension matching, hybrid working, annual training budget.
